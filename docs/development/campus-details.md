# Campus building and sports details

## Scope and evidence

These refinements use public university photographs, mapped footprints and source imagery. Building identity, visible shape and fitted dimensions are recorded separately. They do not establish whole-campus fidelity or current operating status.

The reviewed World Imagery areas report Vantor WV02 imagery dated 2026-01-11, 0.5 m source resolution and 8.47 m absolute accuracy. Roof parallax and manual tracing add uncertainty. Decimal coordinates are implementation precision, not survey accuracy. Source footprints, campus boundary and unrelated details remain intact unless a specific display-only exception is stated below.

## Research Buildings 1 and 2

**Delivery.** Research Building 1 gains a fitted entrance on the reviewed inner-courtyard wall, using the university's photographed glazed entrance, transom and solid canopy as the visual reference. The masonry is cut around the new doorway; the canopy, short steps, ramp and small paved forecourt remain separate geometry. The low north wing and its existing doors remain independent. Research Building 2 retains its existing L-shaped wings, triangular glass courtyard canopy and stone-framed entrance. The canopy's structural glazing grid is retained in aerial views: a thin-bar visibility hint previously hid the entire grid. Geometry, materials and the global visibility policy are unchanged.

**Limits.** Photographs establish Building 1's entrance appearance but do not close its exact position against a surveyed wall corner. Its courtyard axis and dimensions remain fitted. Building 2's entrance compass direction and any additional courtyard entrance remain unverified; the alternate canopy photograph is not sufficient to duplicate an entrance.

Primary references: [Building 1 laboratory emergency drill, 2025-06-20](https://www.lab.pku.edu.cn/info/1021/1831.htm), [materials-school arrival photographs, 2021](https://www.mse.pku.edu.cn/info/1012/1476.htm), [courtyard photographs, 2022](https://www.mse.pku.edu.cn/info/1012/2111.htm), [Building 2 university drill report](https://www.cpc.pku.edu.cn/info/1051/1619.htm), and [School of Life Sciences building photographs](https://www.bio.pku.edu.cn/homes/Index/news_img_jz/68/68.html).

## Dormitories 45, 45A and 45B

**Delivery.** The two existing bicycle canopies between 45A and 45B remain a single independent canopy object, pick 489. A separate visible canopy south of Building 45 is added to Building 45's model. Selecting 45A can display a dashed southeast-region hint for its basement access; the hint is explanatory UI and does not create a physical stair, doorway, hole or route destination.

**Limits.** Official text confirms southeast access to basement services, but not the exact portal, stair direction or ground-level enclosure. The basement contains service businesses, including a bookshop; it is not relabelled as a verified underground library. The new south canopy follows the visible roof extent; its arch, post spacing and height are fitted. A possible shaded western continuation remains unresolved.

## University union Xiaobailou

**Delivery.** A missing independent union building is restored as pick 1339, west of Yannan 62, northeast of Jiayuan dining hall and northwest of Dormitory 31. Its name and searchable aliases identify the university union and Xiaobailou. The model provides a white rectangular mass, pale flat roof and grounded base. Yannan 62 retains its historical identity and footprint.

**Limits.** The official aerial locator supports this site; the roof envelope is image-registered. Height, wall offsets and complete elevations remain fitted or unverified. No unobserved entrance axis or window arrangement is asserted. The former Biology Building is a previous union office location, not an alias for this building.

Primary references: [union new-office aerial locator, 2024-03-06](https://gh.pku.edu.cn/info/1018/14612.htm), [union courtyard photographs, 2024-06-04](https://gh.pku.edu.cn/info/1030/15719.htm), [Xiaobailou refurbishment tender, 2023-06-19](https://jjgcb.pku.edu.cn/zbtb/zbgg/1059jjgcb151034.htm), and [union meeting at Xiaobailou, 2026-04-01](https://news.pku.edu.cn/xwzh/cf6ca481c3794df998e0566d83befa0d.htm).

## Resource Hotel

**Delivery.** Pick 1338 restores the independent L-shaped Resource Hotel using [OSM way 240825484](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/240825484) in the existing coordinate frame. It remains outside the campus boundary and distinct from the south-gate Resource Building. The model preserves the open courtyard and represents the photographed pale walls, red vertical elements and roof towers.

**Limits.** Historical exterior photographs support the overall appearance, not present finishes or complete rear elevations. Door and window positions, tower dimensions and heights are fitted. Current hotel operations are not established. Adding the building does not expand the campus boundary.

Primary identity references: [international-student accommodation guidance](https://isdplus.pku.edu.cn/ORIENTATION/zwb/lhqdzb1/zsap.htm), [university hotel refurbishment tender, 2023](https://jjgcb.pku.edu.cn/zbtb/zbgg/1059jjgcb152055.htm), and [university finance guidance, 2024](https://cese.pku.edu.cn/docs/20240607173600522967.pdf). The appearance reference is a [historical commercial exterior photograph](https://pavo.elongstatic.com/i/tHotel800_600/nw_000dhe3d.jpg); it is not a university-authenticated current survey.

## Qiu gymnasium roof

**Delivery.** Both existing roof leaves are retained. Their inward curl, ridge endpoints and radial sweep are corrected to match the north-up reference. Continuous ridge curves remove the sharp hook from the first fitted revision. Triangle winding follows the revised handedness while preserving the roof subdivisions, dome, structural detailing and underlying building footprint.

**Limits.** Curves and dimensions remain photograph-based fits. Roof-height parallax does not justify moving the whole building or dome. A bright imagery seam is not modelled as architecture; apparent colour highlights do not establish gold cladding.

Primary references: [university construction-department project photographs](https://jjgcb.pku.edu.cn/gcjs/jgxmly/43862.htm), [university description of the paired rotating ridges](https://news.pku.edu.cn/xwzh/129-120849.htm), and [physical-education venue description](https://pe.pku.edu.cn/zhcg/cgjs.htm).

## East sports field

**Delivery.** Two east-west football pitches replace the single north-south display template, retaining detailed markings and goals. The outdoor training-zone display surface follows the visible extent without changing its source parcel. At the existing western climbing site, pick 177, a continuous climbing face and stepped corrugated canopy replace the unsupported narrow tower template. The existing ladder and unrelated site features remain.

**Limits.** Pitch dimensions, climbing-wall height, roof sections, climbing holds and pattern placement are display fits. The unidentified eastern apparatus is not assigned a new identity. The display-surface adjustment is not a revised surveyed boundary.

Primary appearance reference: [university mountaineering report and climbing-wall photographs, 2025-05-30](https://gh.pku.edu.cn/info/1028/16523.htm). The article's event-day wording is inconsistent, so its publication date is not treated as a precise photograph date. North-up imagery supports the paired pitch orientation and relative placement.

## Validation

The final scene cache rebuilt successfully. All 253 runtime/model tests and six Python build/packaging tests passed in a fresh source export restored from the release archive. Its generated website matches the maintained build byte-for-byte. Desktop and 390-pixel-wide WebGL views were inspected for the affected buildings, roofs, canopies, fields and region hint; search and selection of both new buildings were checked. The basement hint was checked through isolation, plan view and clearing selection. Actual WebGL context loss and restoration retained the new scene without application errors.

The sign atlas retains its full 2048-pixel resolution. Changes against the preceding scene are limited to text-edge rasterisation pixels on the existing lake lettering; other atlas pixels and the West Gate plaque components are unchanged. No visual-quality reduction was used.

The fixed-condition comparison completed all 24 phases with stable inputs and owned-process cleanup. All six incremental checks passed the original thresholds; three of six checks against the unchanged frozen reference passed. The remaining global gaps are first-frame submission, campus FPS and lake FPS. These results remain separate from this scoped geometry review; full values and limits are in [Rendering performance](performance.md). Direct local-file runtime, physical-phone behaviour and whole-campus fidelity are not established by these checks. The reference limits above remain open.
