# Campus perimeter

The displayed perimeter distinguishes physical walls, street-facing buildings and controlled entrances from the university area polygon. A road crossing that polygon does not establish a gate. Source map geometry remains intact; placement corrections are separate display fits. Dated references do not establish current access rules.

## Remaining reference limits

- Langrun eastern corner: the source gate opening is kept, but the leaf/booth shape at `node/10768889221` remains obscured. No invented gatehouse.
- Small East Gate: the 2026 eastern turnstile and delivery-cabinet positions are still unresolved; the 2024 canopy outline is not claimed as a complete 2026 reconstruction.
- Northern Qiu Gate: silver scissor metalwork now spans the existing model opening; exact real endpoints, measured width, motor position and return walls are not established. The eastern side entrance also retains uncertain exact width and wall returns; no new stone piers or widened entrance are inferred.
- Southeast Gate: the obscured northern vehicle control and exact placement of the 2026 delivery facilities remain unresolved. Southwest return-wall corners are display fits, not surveyed coordinates.
- Yanyuan Building Gate: trees conceal the northern return fence and its setback. The detailed gate and booths remain corrected; the small adjoining boundary gap is not sealed by an invented straight fence.
- Eastern stretches around the main East Gate/subway structures and gym frontage remain partly obscured. They are not described as newly rebuilt or fully photo-verified.
- Wang Kezhen's short map passage does not independently establish a named campus gate or a rear boundary; no additional arch or guardhouse is placed there.
- Hidden station/main-block facades, exact measured dimensions, detailed South Gate relief and present access arrangements remain unverified.

These limits are retained individually. They do not reopen completed geometry repairs, and they do not close the unrelated building-reference list or the global frozen-performance gaps.

## Validation

The perimeter regression tests cover source-coordinate preservation, station placement and observation framing, wall/gate continuity, openings, source-specific styles, untouched sports runs, unique picks, finite geometry, upward tile normals and transform restoration. A real page-order integration check also verifies that later legacy gate wrappers cannot replace the corrected gate; the detailed leaf geometry is preserved by an exact reflected copy. Rebuild the prepared scene after changing these inputs. Actual desktop and narrow-screen render review and the unchanged frozen comparison remain distinct from unit-test results; [performance](performance.md) records the latter's limits.

The formal comparison passed all six incremental thresholds and three of six frozen thresholds, with the remaining global gaps recorded separately.
